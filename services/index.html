SC.02c — Questions

Before you ask.

How does a project start?

With a 20-minute intro call. If we're a fit, you get a one-page brief and a fixed quote — no long proposals, no surprises later.

Can I hire you for just the film, or just the website?

Yes. Each service stands on its own. Clients often add the others later because the story and the site already share one owner.

How long does a brand film take?

It depends on shoot days and locations. You'll get a dated schedule with the quote, before anything starts.

Do you work remotely?

Strategy, editing and builds happen remotely. Shoots happen on location — travel, if needed, is quoted up front.

What do you need from me?

A clear goal, one decision-maker, and access to the people and places in your story. I handle the rest.

Not sure which one you need? That's what the call is for.
