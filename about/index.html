SC.04 — About

A filmmaker who codes. A founder who tells stories.

For six years I've lived at the seam between narrative and technology. The camera taught me how people feel. The codebase taught me how things work. My job is building in the overlap — so your story and your product never drift apart.

01
The camera

I started with a camera.

Video essays, short films and brand pieces — more than 250 videos produced. My debut short, Existential Evolution, is where I learned that people remember how a story made them feel.

02
The company

Then I started companies.

I co-founded Praatik Hires in 2024 and GrowthGates in 2025, and I run content at Baakhapaa. Founding taught me that a good story is only half the job — it also has to bring in customers.

03
The code

And I learned to build.

As CTO of Sahara Prosthetics (2024–25) I led the engineering behind prosthetics that change daily lives. Code taught me how things work — and how to ship them.

SC.04d — How I work

Three rules I don't break.

01

Feeling first.

If the audience doesn't feel something in the first ten seconds, nothing after it matters.

02

One owner.

You talk to the person doing the work. No hand-offs, no game of telephone between agencies.

03

Measure it.

Every project starts with one number we agree on — views, sign-ups or sales — so we both know what "worked" means.
